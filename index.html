    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Do you issue the ISO certificate?

    No, and you should be wary of anyone who says they do. An independent, UKAS-accredited certification body carries out the certification audit and issues the certificate. We prepare you for it, build the management system, run a mock audit and liaise with the certification body for you.

    How long does ISO certification take?

    It depends on your size, the standard and what you already have in place. The gap analysis gives you a realistic, dated roadmap before you commit to anything further.

    Can you supply our Microsoft licences?

    Yes. We can supply and manage Microsoft 365, Teams Phone, Copilot and other licences on one monthly bill, right-sized so you're not paying for seats or features you don't use.

    We already have an IT provider. Can we still work with you?

    Yes. Many clients keep their existing provider for day-to-day support and use us for security, compliance and projects. We'll work alongside them.

    Who do you work with?

    UK businesses of roughly 20–200 people, with a particular focus on financial services and contact centres. If you're outside that, get in touch anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

    What happens on the free call?

    Twenty minutes to understand what you need and why. If we can help, you'll get a fixed-price proposal. If we can't, we'll say so and point you in the right direction.
